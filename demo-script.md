# HotorBot Demo Script

## Reset between demos

1. Open the **You** tab.
2. Tap **Reset demo** in the upper right.
3. The compare flow, seeded metrics, and matches reset from localStorage.

## 3-minute demo flow

1. Start on **Compare**.
2. Tap each clip once to show the one-audio-at-a-time behavior.
3. Cast 3-5 votes to show instant pair replacement.
4. On every 5th vote, show the reaction chips.
5. On every 10th vote, show the 1-10 winner-strength follow-up.
6. Tap **See who** to show the locked paywall sheet.
7. Open **Matches** and tap **Start 3-minute video date**.
8. End the mock date and show **Meet in person?**
9. Open **You** to show the seeded weekly report.
10. Tap **Reset demo** before handing the device to the next person.

## Offline demo check

1. Open the app once with internet so Safari caches the assets.
2. Wait for the videos to fully load on first pass.
3. Turn on airplane mode.
4. Re-open the app in Safari.
5. The prototype should still run because all assets are local and cached by the service worker.
