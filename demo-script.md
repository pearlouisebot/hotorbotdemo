# Hot or Bot — Demo Script

## Reset
- Tap the "You" tab, scroll to bottom, tap "Reset demo"
- Or in Safari DevTools: Application > Local Storage > clear hotorbot_state

## 3-Minute Demo Flow

1. Open the app (https://hotorbotdemo.vercel.app or local)
2. Compare tab loads automatically with the first pair

### Step 1 — Compare screen (60 sec)
- Show two video clips stacked, both autoplaying muted
- Tap either clip to unmute — the other mutes automatically
- Point out: name, age, distance, verified badge, shared prompt
- Vote using the four buttons: "A, clearly" "B, clearly" "A, barely" "B, barely"
- Show the 150ms fade between pairs
- After 5 votes: attribute chips appear ("What stood out?") — select one and tap Done
- After 10 votes: rating screen appears — tap a number

### Step 2 — Matches tab (45 sec)
- Tap Matches in the bottom nav
- Two pre-seeded matches are shown (Zoe Nakamura and Nadia Okafor)
- Tap a match to open the match card
- Show "You both picked each other"
- Tap "Start 3-minute video date"

### Step 3 — Video date (60 sec)
- Their clip plays full-screen, unmuted
- Icebreaker question at top
- 3:00 countdown runs live
- Show mute/end buttons
- Tap End early to get to "Meet in person?"
- Tap Yes to see the swap-numbers confirmation

### Step 4 — Your Week tab (30 sec)
- Tap the "You" tab
- Show: 68% pick rate, percentile, stacked bar chart
- Show insight card
- Tap the locked "12 people picked you clearly" row — paywall appears
- Tap "Not now" to close

### Step 5 — Paywall (15 sec)
- Tap "See who" in the footer of the Compare screen
- Show the $9.99/month sheet
- Tap "Not now"

## Tips
- Use an iPhone or set browser to 390x844 portrait for the full native feel
- The app works offline once loaded — no internet needed after first load
- Pre-seeded matches are always there even after reset (they come from default state)
